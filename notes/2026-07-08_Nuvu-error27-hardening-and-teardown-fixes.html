PrawnBlaster Quit Crash — root cause

Structural gap in the blacs quit sequence: statemachine timeouts are not stopped before worker shutdown, so a late status_monitor tick can dispatch check_status to the worker after prawnblaster.close() — PortNotOpenError in the worker, then the tab unpacks None and logs CRITICAL. Fixed device-locally in b32c97e (branch fix/prawnblaster-teardown, fast-forwarded into labscript-devices master). Only ever occurred at quit, never during live operation.

Open base-class follow-up (not done): blacs close_tab should call statemachine_timeout_remove_all() before worker shutdown. Any device with a hardware-touching poll has this class of bug — including the base-class 30 s check_remote_values poll.
